Features

Everything you need to stay in control.

Six core modules designed for one thing: making sure you never hit a rate limit by surprise.

01

Activity Rings

Apple Watch-inspired concentric rings for your 5-hour and 7-day usage windows. Separate rings for Sonnet and Opus model breakdown.

02

Pace Indicator

Visual comparison of where you are vs. where you should be. Expected vs. actual usage with clear ahead/behind status at a glance.

03

Burn Rate

5-hour window shown as filling squares. 7-day window as a stock-chart sparkline with expected pace reference line. Color-coded by severity.

04

Smart Alerts

Native macOS notifications at 80% and 95% thresholds on both usage windows. Never hit a rate limit by surprise.

05

Activity Heatmap

24-hour usage pattern grid that builds over time. See exactly when you use Claude most throughout the day.

06

Menu Bar

Live usage percentage in your menu bar. Choose which metrics to display. Four icon themes. Configurable refresh modes.

Setup

Up and running in two minutes.

Three steps. No account creation, no API keys to generate. Just your existing Claude session.

01

Download and open

Grab the latest release or build from source with Xcode.

02

Get your session key

Open claude.ai, grab the sessionKey from your browser's developer tools cookies.

03

Paste and connect

Enter the key in Settings, hit Test, and you're live. Data refreshes automatically.
